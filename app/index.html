Exam interface tutorial

  • Exam structure — the exam has two parts of 100 questions each, with an untimed break between them.
  • Random bank — every attempt draws its 200 questions at random from the 627-question bank across all 7 modules, so each time you open the app the exam is different.
  • Scoring — every question is worth 4 points (800 in total); you need 536 points across both parts to pass.
  • Question & choices — the question and its answer choices appear on screen; select an answer with the mouse.
  • Change answers — select a different choice at any time before the part ends.
  • Navigate — use < Back and Next >, the numbered strip on the left, or the arrow keys.
  • Flag — press Flag to mark a question for review; flagged questions turn red in the review screen.
  • Highlight — select any text in the question, then click the highlighter that appears.
  • Strikethrough — right-click an answer choice to strike it out (right-click again to undo).
  • Section review — the menu icon opens the review screen for the current part: jump to any question and filter by unattempted, attempted, or flagged.
  • Timer — “Section Time Remaining” counts down per part; the part finishes automatically at 00:00:00.
  • Break — after Part 1 you can rest as long as you need; Part 2 starts when you press “Begin Part 2”.
  • Display settings — the settings icon offers alternate color themes, in line with the Prometric tutorial.
